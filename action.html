Prevention & Stewardship

How We Can Slow AMR

Protecting life-saving medicines takes shared action across households, clinics, farms, and communities.

Everyday Steps for Individuals

Use Medicines Responsibly

Only take antibiotics or other antimicrobials prescribed for you. Never share leftover pills or use old prescriptions.

Prevent Infections Early

Wash your hands with soap and water, stay up to date on vaccines, and practice safe food handling to reduce infections in the first place.

The One Health Approach

Human health, animal health, and the environment are closely linked. Slowing AMR requires coordinated action across all three.

Hospitals & Clinics

Strong infection control, accurate lab diagnostics, and antibiotic stewardship programs help target the right drug to the right patient.

Farms & Food Systems

Veterinary oversight, clean housing, and vaccination lower disease rates in animals and cut unnecessary antimicrobial use.

Clean Water & Sanitation

Safe drinking water, sanitation, and proper waste management limit the spread of resistant microbes in communities.
